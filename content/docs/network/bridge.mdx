Use the [Gorbagana Bridge](https://bridge.gorbagana.wtf) when you need to move GOR between Solana and Gorbagana.

## Before you bridge

Check both sides of the transfer before you sign.

**Solana to Gorbagana**

- Source asset: GOR token on Solana
- Destination asset: native GOR on Gorbagana
- Use: fund wallets, apps, and deployments

**Gorbagana to Solana**

- Source asset: native GOR on Gorbagana
- Destination asset: GOR token on Solana
- Use: move back to the Solana token

## Bridge GOR

Follow the bridge UI and verify the destination before signing.

1. Open the [Bridge](https://bridge.gorbagana.wtf).
2. Connect the wallet you want to use.
3. Select the source and destination network.
4. Enter the amount of GOR.
5. Review the route, recipient, and estimated fees.
6. Sign the transaction.
7. Open the transaction in the [Explorer](https://explorer.gorbagana.wtf) after the bridge completes.

## Notes

Keep enough SOL or GOR for fees on the side you are using.

<Callout type="warn" title="Do not submit the same bridge transfer twice">
  If the destination balance does not update immediately, search the transaction or destination address in the explorer before you send another bridge transaction.
</Callout>
